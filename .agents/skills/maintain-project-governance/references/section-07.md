- Accept a portal, adapter or other child gate without promoting its parent stage. Treat acceptance as one ordered transaction: obtain the independent verdict; build the report and receipt from final verified artifacts; recompute every referenced hash; checkpoint the governance files; then update `<stage>/STATE.md`, the acceptance-register row, the handoff's next concrete action and the context-map route. Write both child and parent statuses explicitly, keep the parent at its documented 0%/IN PROGRESS rule, and list the remaining sibling gates. Do not mark the next sibling active in a task tracker or handoff while review, receipt, hash readback or governance reconciliation is still pending; that creates two apparent stages in flight and can strand the unfinished acceptance. Preserve the former state below an explicit historical heading rather than leaving stale “not accepted” instructions mixed with the current status.
